# Paper Writing QA

Read `../../references/writing-qa-rubric.md` and apply only the dimensions
relevant to the section and requested revision.

Verify claims against evidence and citations. Report issues by severity with
exact locations, why they matter, and a concrete correction. Preserve technical
meaning and established terminology.

Do not force every stylistic preference into every section. High-severity
findings are unsupported claims, missing prerequisites, broken formalization,
and citation gaps; minor phrasing preferences remain minor.
